01 / THE ETHICAL AI GUIDE

Six rules for using AI well.

Whether you are writing, studying, or coding, these habits keep you honest, safe, and actually learning. Always follow your own school's AI policy, which may be stricter.

🤝

1. Be Honest

Say when you used AI and how, if your teacher asks for it. Submitting AI work as your own is cheating, even if no one catches it.

🧠

2. Think First

Try the problem yourself before asking AI. Use it to explain, quiz, or give feedback, so you build your own skills and don't skip them.

🔍

3. Verify Everything

AI can state wrong facts confidently and invent sources. Check important claims in textbooks or trusted websites before you rely on them.

🔒

4. Protect Privacy

Never enter passwords, addresses, or other people's personal information. Treat anything you type as something that could be stored.

⚖️

5. Watch for Bias

AI learns from human data and can repeat stereotypes or leave people out. Ask whether an answer is fair, and whose view is missing.

📜

6. Follow the Rules

Rules differ by class and school. If you are unsure whether AI is allowed for an assignment, ask your teacher first.

✅ Responsible uses

Asking AI to explain a concept in simpler words, then summarizing it yourself.
Having AI quiz you before a test.
Asking for feedback on your own draft, then revising it yourself.
Brainstorming topic ideas, then researching your pick.

🚫 Irresponsible uses

Submitting AI-written work as your own.
Using AI during a test or quiz when it is not allowed.
Citing sources the AI gave you without checking they exist.
Making fake images or messages about real people.
